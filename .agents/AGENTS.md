# .agents/AGENTS.md

## How We Work Here

- Keep this folder limited to plans, durable project memory, reusable advisory research, retained evidence, agent-facing design context, and repo-local skills.
- Treat root `AGENTS.md` as the repo-wide operating contract and the nearest local `AGENTS.md` as the subtree authority.
- Create a durable plan only when `.agents/PLANS.md` requires one, and store it under `.agents/execplans/`.
- Keep active task progress in the relevant plan's checkboxes instead of mirroring it elsewhere.
- Keep accepted project knowledge in `../docs/` or its established specification owner. `research/` and `evidence/` do not create product authority.
- Link reusable research and retained evidence from each consuming plan instead of copying raw material into plans, design context, or project docs.

## Boundaries

- `research/` owns reusable synthesized analysis that future plans can consult.
- `evidence/` owns justified screenshots, logs, measurements, render comparisons, model output, and other observed-state proof.
- `design/DESIGN.md` maps agents to approved design authority and production owners; it is not an asset archive.
- `skills/` contains only repeated workflows with their own trigger, stop conditions, or artifact contract.
- `tmp/` is non-authoritative working material. Do not cite it as product truth or durable evidence.
