# Pixel Space-Hangar Reference Gallery

## Purpose

Keep the visual evidence behind the MVP comparison visible without copying
proprietary game art into the repository. Game images below are loaded from
official Steam-hosted URLs and link to their official store pages. Local files
are limited to the CC0 packs recorded with source, license, and checksum in
[`reference-manifest.json`](./reference-manifest.json).

This is a reference gallery, not an asset-approval list. No external art in this
folder is imported by Godot.

### Downloaded CC0 samples

[![CC0 asset contact sheet](./references/cc0/cc0-reference-contact-sheet.png)](./references/cc0/cc0-reference-contact-sheet.png)

The local subset covers small ships, engine frames, modular tiles, background
tile structure, and UI frames. Primary source pages:

- [Kenney — Pixel Shmup](https://www.kenney.nl/assets/pixel-shmup)
- [OpenGameArt — Top-down space fighter sprite](https://opengameart.org/content/top-down-space-fighter-sprite)
- [OpenGameArt — 2D spaceship sprites with engines](https://opengameart.org/content/2d-spaceship-sprites-with-engines)
- [OpenGameArt — Sci-fi platform tiles](https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-platform-tiles)
- [OpenGameArt — Sci-Fi background tilemap](https://opengameart.org/content/sci-fi-backgroud-tilemap)
- [OpenGameArt — Assets: UI Minimalism SciFi](https://opengameart.org/content/assets-ui-minimalism-scifi)

## Findings

- Small Kenney ships are the strongest local reference for immediate facing and
  role-readable silhouettes. Their motifs and palette are not the target.
- The downloaded platform tiles are a useful rejection example: side-view
  shadows, mottled materials, vents, and repeated panel noise do not survive a
  high-count top-down combat scene.
- The downloaded UI frames are license-safe but visually too dark, textured, and
  border-heavy. Only their spacing and scalable-frame structure are relevant.
- The fighter and engine sheets show why black outlines and many shaded colors
  should not define the production grammar.
- Across the official game references, the reusable pattern is not one specific
  art style. It is quiet walkable space, one blocker language, compact role
  silhouettes, distinct projectile ownership, and restrained corner HUD.

## Limitations

- Steam headers are marketing images; conclusions are cross-checked across
  multiple titles and limited to named visual problems.
- CC0 status makes an asset legally reusable, not visually suitable.
- Proprietary reference images remain remote and must never become direct
  production inputs or copied sprite geometry.
