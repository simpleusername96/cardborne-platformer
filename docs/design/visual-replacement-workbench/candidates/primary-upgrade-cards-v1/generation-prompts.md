# Generation Prompts

All three calls used the built-in ImageGen tool and supplied
`docs/design/cardborne-universal-art-style-reference.png` as the actual reference image.

Shared contract:

> Create Cardborne upgrade-card content artwork for later mechanical conversion to a
> 192x192 transparent PNG. The supplied sheet is style grammar only; do not reproduce its
> objects or layout. Use one dominant warm-off-white flat-stencil silhouette, one amber
> mechanic accent, one short near-black offset shadow, hard antialiased edges, a centered
> composition, and a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma background. No text, numbers,
> badge, ring, card frame, gradient, bevel, glow, material rendering, particles,
> decorative background, tiny circles, repeated lamps, seams, or watermark.

Miss Compensation:

> Show four small missed projectile trails passing cleanly to either side of a target
> gap and converging into one much larger, forceful next projectile through the center.
> Make stored misses and the strengthened next hit unmistakable. Avoid crosshair and
> target-designator metaphors.

Hit Chain:

> Show four aligned projectile impacts connected by one continuous forward rhythm and
> ending in a stronger fifth impact. Communicate consecutive successful hits building
> power. Do not depict electricity, literal metal chain links, a combo counter, or shield.

Braced Fire:

> Show a compact top-down weapon platform settling into a stable firing stance: two broad
> stabilizer feet planted outward, one centered forward barrel, and one strong straight
> muzzle stroke. Communicate movement charged first and stationary stabilized fire second
> without arrows, speed lines, text, or a vehicle copied from the reference.
