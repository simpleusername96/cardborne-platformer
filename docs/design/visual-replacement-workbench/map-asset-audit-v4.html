Three floor directions, one shared structure kit

Only the floor master changes between directions. The outer wall, in-game wall, and terrain obstacle are deliberately shared instead of generating three redundant copies. All use a near-black space exterior, a cool neutral-gray hangar deck, brighter pale-gray walls, dark separation, and isolated lower cover. There are no item, facility, rail, light, decal, or decorative-effect changes.
