Enemy review

Threats should be fair before they are busy.

Visible enemies need clear local warnings. Offscreen events need clear limits.

Current factsAttack rule lockedApproved directionNot implemented
Approved direction, not implemented.

Normal attacks still use range and line of sight without a body-visible gate. Current fixed roles are still enemies. The product spec and runtime must change before these new rules are true.

Three priorities

  1. Make attacks fair: add the normal attack visibility gate.
  2. Make stages learnable: reduce mobile role pressure per stage.
  3. Make world danger neutral: convert fixed hostile roles into devices that can hit both teams.

Locked rules

Attack gate · locked
  1. Offscreen: move only.
  2. Visible: show the body plus a small margin, then check clear line of sight.
  3. Give a local warning.
  4. Once an attack starts, it may finish.
Ownership · locked

Fixed hostile roles become neutral devices. Map owns their placement and lifetime.

Normal visible enemies get no route UI. Route UI is only for boss or large offscreen neutral events.

Role pressure

Target: no more than 4 mobile roles per stage. This is direction, not a locked rule. The “two familiar + one new” mix is an example, not a final rule.

Fixed-role conversion

The device is not an enemy type, stage target, or XP reward. Its attack can hurt the player, enemies, and bosses. If it kills an enemy, G1 still gives stage progress and XP.
