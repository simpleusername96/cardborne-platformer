Enemies

Make attacks fair before adding more enemy types.

The player should learn a small threat set and see a normal attack before it starts.

1 · Problem

Too much arrives at once

  • Some attacks can begin outside the screen.
  • Later stages use too many enemy jobs.
  • Fixed attackers act like extra enemy types.

2 · Fix

Small, fair stage sets

  • Off-screen enemies move toward the player but do not attack.
  • Use at most four mobile jobs in one stage.
  • Replace jobs slowly between stages.

3 · Decided

Threat rules

  • The enemy body must be visible before attack start.
  • Normal attacks use a local warning, not a long route line.
  • Fixed attackers become world objects that can hurt both sides.

4 · Your choice

No choice now

I can make the first five stage lists and tune the visible margin and warning time.
