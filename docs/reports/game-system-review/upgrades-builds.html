Upgrades

Every choice should change how you play.

Weak number changes and repeated jobs should be cut or merged.

1 · Problem

Many choices do not matter

  • “Attack a device first” is too narrow.
  • A wider stun area alone is not a build.
  • Some bonuses repeat items or other upgrades.

2 · Fix

Change behavior

  • Change aim, movement, timing, target choice, or weapon form.
  • Merge repeated jobs.
  • Show each build limit on the choice card.

3 · Decided

Two rules

  • If a world danger kills an enemy, it gives normal progress and experience points.
  • Each build group has a maximum count.
  • Cards must not copy a pickup or a standing pad.

4 · Your choice

No choice now

I can rewrite, merge, remove, and test the first set. Review it after it is playable.

Keep or remove

One simple test

An upgrade stays only if the player can feel it soon and it changes aim, movement, timing, target choice, or weapon form. If it only adds a small number or repeats another reward, remove or merge it.
