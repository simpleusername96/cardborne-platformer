Map & world

Keep the field open. Make every object clear.

The player should keep moving, find short safety, and understand every map object without a new HUD marker.

Approved direction, not implemented.

These are design and UI rules. They do not change the current map, assets, collision, or runtime. Rejected sheets are archival evidence only.

Three priorities

  1. Open movement: no combat dead end; each pocket has more than one wide exit.
  2. Trusted safety: cover blocks fire and rushes for a short time. It never becomes a route wall.
  3. Clear world tools: neutral devices and pickup items create a quick choice while the player keeps moving.

What the current generator does

Seeded

It is not pure noise

The run seed selects the field and places 8 cover rectangles. Clearance and reach checks already exist.

Stage objects

Many jobs share one layout

The current stage plan includes 4 fixed hostile roles, 6 pickups, 8 crates, and 12 saved support positions.

Main failure

Safe can still feel wrong

Reward enclosures create closed pockets. Pads reward waiting. Crates stay where the stage placed them.

AS-IS → approved TO-BE

AS-IS

Current map

Support pads are live circular areas. One wall PNG serves both outer and inner walls. Some layouts can form closed reward pockets.

Approved TO-BE

Movement first

Use sparse cover, open escape sides, fixed trusted safety, neutral devices, and instant items. Retire repair and overdrive pads from the first redesign.

Locked rules

Cover is short safety

It blocks fire and direct rushes. It does not draw a travel lane.

Free movement stays free

Do not make a maze, narrow trap, or dead end. A combat space needs more than one wide escape side.

This page uses G1

The overview owns the G1 score rule. Map sends an enemy defeat to that rule. Breaking the device itself sends no enemy defeat.

Support-pad rule conflict

Current visual rules

Pads are allowed

VISUAL_SYSTEM.md permits shared circular repair and overdrive pads at their live radius.

Approved game direction

Pads reward waiting

Remove repair and overdrive pads from the first redesign. Keep movement-friendly pickups instead.

The contract must change before implementation. Do not silently keep shared rings or support pads because the current visual contract permits them.

Neutral world package

Keep two current mechanics separate

Arc Surge and Wear Collapse

They may share neutral targeting, warnings, and G1 score rules. They do not become one mechanic. Arc is a chosen stage danger. Wear changes state and stays changed through the run.

First test package

Three moving-combat choices

  • Gravity Core: break it to pull enemies for a short time.
  • Cryo Cell: break it to freeze nearby enemies.
  • Meteor Patrol: a neutral attack moves on a fully shown route.

A neutral device is not an enemy type. Breaking it gives no kill and no XP. Its attack can hurt the player, enemies, and bosses.

What Codex can decide

  • Seeded, safe placement for sparse cover and items.
  • First counts, timers, and neutral-device mix.
  • Neutral body, danger edge, and no enemy marker.
  • Keep Repair and XP Recall; test Dash Cell and Decoy Charge.

Your later visual choice

Choose the final map asset family after two or more real-size candidates show a whole field, each asset alone, repeat joins, long wall stretch, and all damage states. Parts may be mixed across candidates.

External assets are allowed only with a clear license, source URL, saved source proof, and approved PNG adaptation.

Show later world ideas and clear rejects

Later tests

Decoy Beacon pulls enemy attention. Volatile Battery makes a shared blast. Shock Link joins two devices with a dangerous line.

Do not build

No more waiting pads. No second EMP-like device. No solid wall that moves during a fight.

Box relocation

  1. Start with a fixed box count.
  2. After a timer, choose one unopened normal box.
  3. Move it only when off-screen and not being hit.
  4. Use a reachable saved position far from the player and recent boxes.
  5. Show a short arrival warning. Never create, refill, or move a reward-room box.

Map UI rules

World truth

Show the exact live area before neutral damage. Show the full next path for a meteor. Use a short arrival cue for a moved box.

Map language

Neutral devices are world objects, not red enemies. The minimap keeps only player, item, enemy, and boss marker roles.

Transit state

The gate itself shows its pair, active state, and cooldown. Do not add another permanent HUD box.
