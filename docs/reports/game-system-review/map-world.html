Map

Keep movement free. Make cover and danger clear.

The map needs fewer visual parts, safer random placement, and no reason to stand still.

1 · Problem

Busy and unclear

  • The floor repeats too loudly.
  • Outer wall and inner cover look too similar.
  • Closed pockets and standing pads fight fast movement.

2 · Fix

Open rule-based maps

  • Use a quiet large surface.
  • Place a few isolated covers and reject blocked layouts.
  • Move normal boxes only to safe off-screen spots.

3 · Decided

Rules stay fixed

  • No maze, dead end, or enclosed reward pocket.
  • Cover gives short safety. It does not form a route.
  • No heal or attack-boost pad. Gates and shared danger stay.
  • Fixed enemy objects become world objects that hurt both sides.

What changes, and when

  1. Stage start
    Create one valid open layout. Cover, gates, and normal ground danger stay in place for that stage.
  2. During the stage
    A normal unopened box may move to a safe off-screen spot. A meteor may travel only on a path shown before it hurts anything.
  3. Next stage
    Create a new layout and a new small mix of devices.

Two ground dangers stay separate

The fixed electric strip stays one danger. The floor that cracks and later hurts stays another. They share fair warnings, but they do not become one object and they do not move.

First new world actions

Break one device to pull enemies. Break another to freeze nearby enemies. A moving meteor attacks both sides on a fully shown path.
