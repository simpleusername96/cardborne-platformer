Cardborne / incremental improvement brief

Improve the shooter without rebuilding Cardborne.

Keep the current field, eight-cycle quota flow, bosses, cards, upgrade modal, controls, and performance limits. Improve the existing systems in small slices: readable HUD, useful tactical guidance, fewer early interruptions, and slightly more relevant enemy pressure.

The player stays fast and the game stays recognizable. Each change must prove its value before the next one begins.
