Cardborne · Game Design Reset

Keep moving. Use cover. Turn the field against enemies.

This proposal starts from the real play style. The player moves fast, aims by hand, and fights a crowd. The field should give short safety, clear risks, and tools that work while the player keeps moving.

01 · Main decision

The field must support motion, not waiting.

Repair and attack pads ask the player to stay in a small area. The rest of the game asks the player to keep moving. This is a rule conflict, not an art problem.

Keep

Fast free movement

The player should cross the field quickly. The map should not become a route puzzle.

Keep

Cover as short safety

Cover blocks shots and direct rushes. It gives a short break. It does not tell the player where to travel.

Remove first

Repair and attack pads

They reward waiting, overlap with items and upgrades, and make a hurt player an easy target.

Build around

Neutral world tools

Teleport, shared hazards, and breakable devices create choices while the player stays in motion.

This proposal conflicts with the current product specification. The current specification requires repair fields, overdrive fields, four fixed hostile units per stage, and a 41-card upgrade catalog. Approve the new rules and update that specification before implementation.

AS-IS

  • Two repair fields and two attack fields run every stage.
  • Five fixed roles are treated as enemies.
  • Arc Surge and collapsed floor already hurt both teams.
  • Many cards add a number but do not change a play decision.

TO-BE

  • No field effect asks the player to stand still for value.
  • Fixed attackers become neutral world devices.
  • Both neutral types share the same team and warning rules. They still do different jobs.
  • Upgrades change aim, movement, timing, or target choice.

02 · Open field and cover

Cover gives a dodge break. It must not make a corridor.

The earlier report said walls should make movement paths. That was wrong. Cardborne needs a wide combat field with sparse blockers and no dead ends.

Hard field rules

RuleReasonSimple test
No dead endsA crowd can turn a pocket into an unavoidable death.Every open area has at least two wide exit sides.
No long internal wall chainA wall chain turns free combat into a route puzzle.Internal blockers stay separate and leave wide gaps.
Cover blocks real dangerCover is useful only when shots, sight, and direct bodies use the same blocker.Player, enemy, shot, sight, and warning tests agree.
Keep boss space openA large boss and a large crowd need room to turn.The boss can pass around every major cover group.
No art-owned collisionThe picture must not add an invisible or false wall.Debug collision and the visible mass match at game size.
Breakable Bulkhead: remove its current job as the only entrance to a reward pocket. That job creates a dead end and makes structure damage a narrow chore. Reuse the destructible-object code for open-space tactical devices such as the Gravity Core or Cryo Cell.

03 · What changes when

Do not move solid cover during a fight.

Change content at a clear time. A moving hazard can move because its path is the rule. A wall should not move because the player uses it as trusted safety.

World partChosenCan move inside a stage?Recommended life

Large cover islandsAt run startNoKeep for the full run so safety stays trusted.
Wear Collapse TileAt run startNoKeep its position and state for the full run.
Arc SurgeAt stage startNoPick a safe strip position for the stage. It cycles in place.
Transit Gate pairAt stage startNoKeep both ends fixed until the stage ends.

Unopened normal boxAt stage startYes, off-screen onlyMove one existing box after a timer. Do not make a new reward.

Direct answer: the two current neutral terrain types should not both move. Arc Surge should be picked again at a stage boundary and stay fixed during that stage. Wear Collapse should stay fixed for the full run and change only its state. A meteor is a separate moving event with a full warning path.

04 · Neutral terrain

Give both neutral terrain types the same basic rules.

Arc Surge and Wear Collapse do different jobs. Keep both. They should share who they can hit, how warnings work, and how the result is counted.

Current fact

Arc Surge

A fixed rectangle warns, turns on, and hits each actor once in that active window. It can hurt the player, normal enemies, and bosses.

Recommended proposal

New place: scheduled neutral line hazard.

Current fact

Wear Collapse Tile

Actor entries add wear. The third entry collapses the tile. A collapsed tile hurts the player, normal enemies, and bosses on entry and while they stay.

Recommended proposal

New place: run-long neutral terrain that changes state.

Shared neutral rules

Shared ruleRecommended result
TeamNeutral. It is not a red enemy and not a player skill.
Valid targetsPlayer, normal enemy, and boss. A special device can list a clear exception.
WarningShow the exact live area before damage. A moving event shows its next path.
ScoreDamage is recorded as environment damage, not player weapon damage.
Enemy deathRecommended: it still advances the stage kill count and drops normal experience.
Map markerDo not use an enemy marker. Show it as explored world geometry only.
ArtNeutral gray body, clear danger edge, and one simple shape for its rule.
Important balance rule: a boss must not be erased by parking it inside a neutral hazard. Use lower boss damage, a per-event limit, or short resistance. Keep the rule visible and the same for every boss.

05 · Fixed enemies

Move every fixed hostile role out of the enemy roster.

The current game has five fixed hostile roles. They do not chase the player, but they use enemy art, enemy markers, and enemy rules. Turn them into neutral devices that can also hit enemies.

Current roleNew world roleNew ruleWhy
TurretNeutral SentryAfter a clear lock, shoot the nearest visible actor from either team.The player can drag enemies into its fire.
MineNeutral Trigger MineAny actor can trigger it. A player shot can set it off early. The blast hurts both teams.It becomes a timing tool instead of a red enemy.
Interceptor TowerNeutral Counter SentryMerge it into the Sentry family. It reacts to nearby firing and locks the shooter from either team.This reduces world-role count and keeps a different trigger.

System rules after conversion

  • Neutral devices do not count as enemy types.
  • They do not add to the stage kill target.
  • They do not drop experience when destroyed.
  • Enemy and player shots can damage them unless the device rule says otherwise.
  • Their attacks can hurt both teams.
  • They use neutral world art, not red enemy craft art.
  • Only an off-screen or very large device attack needs a long path warning.
This is not a color swap. Current projectiles are split into player and enemy paths. A true neutral shot or beam needs a new target system that can hit either side. The conversion must also remove enemy kill-count, experience, minimap, guidebook, and installation-stat behavior. Boss objective pylons are not part of this conversion; they stay owned by the boss system.

06 · New terrain ideas

Add tools that change the fight while the player moves.

A good terrain object creates a short plan: shoot it now, lead enemies near it, cross it, avoid it, or save it. It should not ask the player to stand inside a circle.

First test

Gravity Core

Trigger: break the core.

Effect: nearby enemies are pulled to the broken core for a short time.

Use: group enemies for a beam, EMP, mine, or boss attack.

First test

Cryo Cell

Trigger: break the cell.

Effect: nearby normal enemies freeze. Bosses slow for a shorter time.

Use: make a short escape window without standing still.

First test

Meteor Patrol

Trigger: selected stage event.

Effect: a meteor moves through the field and hurts both teams.

Use: read the shown path and pull enemies into it.

Later test

Decoy Beacon

Trigger: shoot or touch it.

Effect: nearby mobile enemies chase it for a short time, then it breaks.

Risk: too strong if every boss can be fully distracted.

Later test

Volatile Battery

Trigger: break it.

Effect: a large delayed blast hurts every actor.

Use: time the break, then leave before the blast.

Later test

Shock Link

Trigger: damage one of two linked nodes.

Effect: a warned line fires between the nodes and hurts both teams.

Risk: keep only one active link so the field stays readable.

Ideas to reject for now

Another support pad

It repeats the same waiting problem.

Another EMP device

It overlaps the player's only active skill.

A moving solid wall

It can erase trusted cover and create a dead end during combat.

07 · Items and boxes

Items should reward travel at the moment of contact.

Items work better than support pads because the player can collect them and keep moving. New items should solve a short combat need, not copy a permanent upgrade.

Recommended item set

  • Repair Capsule: keep. It gives instant hull and ends.
  • Experience Recall: keep. It moves rewards to the moving player.
  • Dash Cell: test. It refreshes dash on contact.
  • Decoy Charge: test. It leaves a short lure at the pickup point while the player moves away.
  • Barrier Charge: hold. It may overlap too much with defense upgrades.
  • Attack Boost: reject for now. It repeats overdrive and passive damage cards.

Box relocation rule

  1. The stage starts with a fixed number of boxes.
  2. After a timer, choose one unopened normal box.
  3. Move it only when it is off-screen and not being hit.
  4. Use a reachable saved position far from the player and recent box positions.
  5. Show a short arrival warning at the new position.
  6. Never create a new box and never refill an opened box.

08 · Upgrade reset

A clean card cannot fix a weak choice.

The current catalog has 41 cards. Some cards change play. Many cards only raise a number, repeat another system, or depend on a narrow object.

13 of 41

Number-only cards

They add no new interaction. A number can help balance, but it should not pretend to be a new build.

Hidden cost

Two optional slots

Four optional secondary weapons share only two slots. The card UI does not show used or free slots.

Missing UI

Behavior row never appears

The product rule asks for a “New behavior” row. Current offer data never gives the UI the difference needed to show it.

Weak identity

No clear main build

Elements and many other paths can all stack. The current rule meant to stop some combinations is not used.

Every future card must pass four tests

  1. The player can see or feel it soon after choosing it.
  2. It matters in normal combat, not only near one rare object.
  3. It changes aim, movement, timing, or target choice.
  4. It does not repeat an item, pad, or another card.

Clear current problems

Current card or groupProblemRecommended action

EMP FocusMore damage and more radius do not create a new EMP decision.Replace raw size with a behavior: expose stunned targets, pull shots, or create a short safe break.
Relay OverloadIt only disables support units and installations for longer.Remove after neutral-device conversion.
Mass Driver structure damageExtra structure damage matters only for a narrow object set.Remove the structure-only part. Keep a clear heavy-shot branch if its shot feel is strong.

Good foundations to keep or rebuild

Shot shape

Forked Muzzle, Phase Lance, and Ricochet change where the player aims and how enemies should line up.

Dash action

Ion Wake, Ram Pulse, Phase Shear, and Coolant Surge make the timing and path of dash matter.

Secondary identity

Seeker, Ion Field, Orbit Blades, Wake Mines, and Escort Drone create visible build types.

Target mark

Marked Salvo gives the manual gun and automatic Seeker one shared target decision.

Element behavior

Burn, poison, and chill can stay if each branch changes how the player chooses a target.

Direct movement

Tuned Thrusters and Dash Capacitor are simple, but they support the game's main action and are easy to feel.

Proposed upgrade lanes

LaneQuestion for the playerExample choices
Main gun styleHow do I line up and control targets?Precision, Barrage, or Heavy/Siege. Pick one clear main identity.
Hit effectWhat extra result does a hit create?Burn, poison, or chill. Pick one root, then evolve it.
Seeker target ruleWhat should the built-in Seeker hunt?Closest threat, marked target, or a clearly defined world target.
Dash formWhat does my escape path do?Damage trail, arrival pulse, target mark, or short fire-rate window.
EMP formWhat do I do after I press EMP?Expose targets, gain one earned barrier, or create a second smaller pulse.
Extra weapon slotWhich automatic weapon supports my movement?Choose up to two optional systems. Show “slot 1 of 2” before the choice.
Tactical responseWhat short weakness does this run need help with?Hull, movement, cooldown, or pickup comfort. Use rarely, not as a forced choice seat.
Recommended offer shape: show one new main style or extra weapon, one upgrade for something already owned, and one short-term aid. If movement speed is required for the game to feel fair, raise the base ship speed. Do not force Tuned Thrusters into an apparent choice.

Upgrade card UI direction

  • Keep the calm three-card layout.
  • Show the behavior change before secondary numbers.
  • Show at most the values needed to compare the choice.
  • Show optional secondary capacity on every module card, such as “slot 1 of 2.”
  • Show real before-and-after behavior, such as “side shots 1 → 2” or “pierce 1 → 2.”
  • Use different art only for different behavior.
  • Do not give two unrelated rules the same shield or ring image.
  • Make the three offers answer different questions. Do not show three small stat increases together.

09 · Stage example

Enemy change and world change should be gradual.

Fixed devices no longer count as enemy types. Each stage can use a small mobile enemy set and a small neutral world set. This table is an example, not final balance data.

StageMobile enemy setNeutral world setNew lesson
1Scrap Drone, Chaser, ShooterTrigger Mine, one Arc SurgeLearn chase, range, and one shared hazard.
2Chaser, Shooter, ControllerNeutral Sentry, Gravity CoreLead enemies into device fire and group them.
3Shooter, Rammer, Bulkhead GuardSweep Emitter, Cryo CellRead a long warning and make an escape window.
4Rammer, Bulkhead Guard, Repair TenderMeteor event, Trigger MineFight around a moving neutral attack.
5Bulkhead Guard, Splitter Barge, Repair Tender, one familiar roleTwo known neutral systemsCombine learned rules. Do not add another new device.
Stage rule: introduce at most one new enemy idea and one new world idea at the same time. Later stages become harder through combinations, not through ten simultaneous role names.

10 · Recommended change order

Change rules before art.

This order avoids making assets for systems that may be removed.

P0

Approve the new product rules

Decide support-pad removal, open-field rules, which system controls neutral devices, hazard life, box relocation, and the upgrade cut rule.

P1

Update the product specification

Make the new design the main rule source. The current specification still requires the old systems.

P2

Remove the two support pad schedules

Remove repair and overdrive fields, their guide entries, and their world art use. Keep repair pickup and build upgrades separate.

P3

Create one neutral world system

Give neutral terrain and devices the same basic target, warning, damage, score, life, and movement rules.

P4

Convert fixed enemies

Move their stage placement, combat targeting, guide category, markers, and art out of enemy systems.

P5

Rebuild map and item change

Remove dead ends, validate open cover, add safe box relocation, and test one moving meteor event.

P6

Cut and rebuild upgrades

Start with behavior-changing cards. Remove narrow and duplicate cards before tuning values.

P7

Make new simple visual tests

Show each approved role at real size and in real gameplay use. Do not return to the old isolated candidate-sheet method.

Choices still needed

Gravity affects whom?

Recommended first test: enemies only. A harder true-neutral version can pull the player too.

Do environment kills drop experience?

Recommended: yes. The player should not lose progress for smart terrain use.

Can enemies use Transit Gates?

Recommended first test: no. Keep the gate as a clear escape and reposition tool.

How many neutral devices per stage?

Recommended: start small. Use only enough types that every warning is clear in a crowd.
